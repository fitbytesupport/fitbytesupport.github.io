Common questions

How do I report an ad?

In the app, go to Settings > Report an ad, or email us. Tell us what the ad showed, and include a screenshot if you have one.

How do I cancel Premium?

Go to iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > FitByte. You can also get there from Settings > Manage subscription in the app. Apple handles all billing and refunds.

I reinstalled the app and lost Premium

Go to Settings > Restore purchases in the app, using the same Apple account you subscribed with.

How many scans do I get?

Photo scans are limited to 10 a day on the free plan and 50 a day on Premium. Barcode scans are unlimited.

The numbers look wrong

Photo scans are estimates, so you can edit anything before saving. For packaged food, scan the barcode or take a clear photo of the nutrition label. FitByte isn't medical advice, so talk to a doctor or dietitian about your diet or health.

A barcode wasn't found

Barcode data comes from Open Food Facts, a public database that doesn't include every product. Take a photo of the nutrition label instead.

How do I delete my data?

Your meals are stored on your phone. Delete individual meals in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. See the Privacy Policy for more.
