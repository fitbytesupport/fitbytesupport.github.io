Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The short version. FitByte has no user accounts, and your meal log stays on your phone. Some things have to leave your phone for the app to work: photos you scan go to Google's AI, barcodes go to Open Food Facts, and the free version shows ads from Google. We don't sell your data.

What stays on your phone

Your meals, goals, saved meal photos, language and daily scan count are stored only on your device. FitByte doesn't run a server that keeps a copy.

What leaves your phone, and why

Photos you scan (Google Gemini)

When you take a photo to scan, a smaller copy is sent to Google's Gemini AI to work out the food and its nutrition. The camera is only used while the scanner is open, and only the photo you take is sent.

Google says it doesn't use this content to improve its products under the paid service we use. Google logs prompts and responses for up to 55 days to detect misuse and meet legal requirements, and authorised Google staff may review flagged content. Please don't photograph people or private documents with the scanner.

Barcodes (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to look up the product. Like any web request, this includes your device's IP address. Open Food Facts doesn't receive your meal log.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your IP address, approximate location, advertising identifier and how you interact with ads. If you're in the UK, EEA or Switzerland, you'll be asked for consent first, and on iPhone your advertising identifier is only available if you allow tracking when asked. Premium members don't see ads.

You can read how Google uses data from apps that show its ads. To change tracking at any time, go to iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat)

Premium is purchased through Apple, which handles your payment. We never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription, using a random app ID and your purchase history.

Emails to us

If you email support or report an ad, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use them only to reply and to deal with your report.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • We don't use your meal log or nutrition goals for advertising.
  • We don't ask for your name or create an account for you.

Deleting your data

You can delete any meal in the app. Deleting FitByte from your phone removes everything it stored there. Data already sent to the services above is handled under their own policies, and Google's AI logs expire as described above. To cancel Premium, go to iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the UK and EU, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict how it's used. Email us and we'll help. Because we don't keep your meal log on a server, there's usually nothing further for us to retrieve or delete. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Age

FitByte is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
